# D3 Audit: Real vs Synthetic Laughter in WavLM Space
Generated by scripts/audit_d3.py. Data: embeddings/d3_multi_features.parquet (682 rows), embeddings/d3_multi_inventory.csv.

## Bottom line

**The AUC≈1.0 "real vs synthetic laughter" claim is NOT trustworthy as stated.** It is very
likely riding recording-pipeline / clip-construction confounds (duration, silence padding,
loudness, spectral tilt) rather than any laughter-specific property WavLM has learned, for
three converging reasons:

1. A **content-free 8-scalar probe** (duration, lead/trail silence, RMS, spectral centroid,
   spectral rolloff, ZCR, HF-energy-ratio — nothing about the sound "being laughter") already
   gets **AUC 0.83-0.98** real-vs-synth and **AUC 0.98** for speech-real-vs-laugh-real, using
   plain logistic regression, no WavLM at all.
2. After the fix-stack (2.0s center-crop to equalize length, peak-normalize to equalize
   loudness, then WavLM + PCA-10), WavLM AUC **drops from ~1.00 to 0.83-1.00** and in 4/5
   methods is statistically indistinguishable from (or below) the content-free scalar
   baseline on the same fixed clips. Only bark_laughter_token shows WavLM modestly
   exceeding the content-free probe (0.867 vs 0.833, n=30, within noise).
3. The **speech-real vs laugh-real "sanity" AUC of 1.0 survives every control**, including the
   full fix-stack and PCA-10 (still 1.000). Since speech and laughter differ in every possible
   real way (this is not surprising or a confound by itself), this shows the probe/embedding
   pipeline is highly sensitive to ANY large group-level difference — which is exactly why an
   AUC of 1.0 on real-vs-synth-laughter, by itself, is weak evidence for a laughter-authenticity
   signal specifically. It is at least as consistent with corpus/recording-pipeline separability.

Labels-shuffled and real-vs-real split controls both correctly collapse to chance (~0.50),
so the classifier is not systematically overfitting given genuinely null labels — the
concern here is specifically **which real signal separates the true labels**, and the
evidence points to non-laughter confounds (duration/silence/loudness/spectral-tilt), not
classifier-level overfitting.

**Required fix before publishing the "AUC≈1.0 across 4 generators" headline:** reframe as
"real vs synthetic laughter clips are separable in WavLM space" (true, robust) rather than
"WavLM encodes laughter authenticity" (not supported — separability tracks duration/loudness/
silence-padding confounds at least as strongly as it tracks WavLM content). Concretely: (a)
report the content-free-probe AUC alongside every WavLM AUC as a floor/baseline; (b) always
run the length-matched + peak-normalized ablation and report that number as the headline,
not the raw-clip number; (c) do not claim "no laughter-content leakage" for methods where
WavLM(fixed) <= content-free (bark_laughs_inline, audioldm2, parler_tts, xtts all fall in
this bucket below).
