● Done. I built and ran a 10-audit red-team suite in experiments/axis_audits/,
  with all deliverables in audits_outputs/: one folder per audit (summary .md +
  CSVs + figures + results JSON), the roll-up COMPREHENSIVE_VERDICT.md, and
  comprehensive_verdict.pdf. Everything was recomputed independently from the
  cached embeddings/logits — not from the original analysis code.

  What is proven beyond reasonable scrutiny

  - The core law (sd_along → MLAAD hardness) is real and very strong. The
  headline 0.273/p=0.0005 turned out to be hardcoded in build_package.py with no
  artifact behind it — but my from-scratch recomputation reproduces it (LOSO
  R²=0.277, perm p=0.0002, ρ=+0.597). It's the only test of 83 ever recorded in 
  the project that survives global BH-FDR, and it survives leave-one-system
  jackknife (0/61 folds lose significance), n_utts/RMS/language/vel_entropy
  partials, estimator swaps, and an explicit winner's-curse simulation (wins 77%
  of discovery halves, confirms at p<0.05 in 99.2%). Stricter utterance
  thresholds strengthen it (ρ→0.69) — the signature of a true effect attenuated
  by noise.
  - Axis rotation is real, and stronger than claimed: each corpus axis is nearly
  noiseless (split-half cos 0.93–0.98) yet cross-corpus cosines sit at the
  768-d chance floor — and MLAAD↔ASVspoof21 is actually negative.
  - Domain-conditional agreement is supported by evidence the paper missed:
  AASIST-ZS~RobustGoat (both ASVspoof-trained) is a second same-domain
  cross-architecture pair at 0.544, matching 0.553. Caveat: no individual
  same-vs-cross difference is significant at n=61 (p=0.06–0.22) — claim the
  pattern, not a significant gap.
  - I7 MLAAD fusion (0.272→0.163) is clean: negative ΔEER in all 5 folds × 3
  seeds, and fusion beats both components. The hardness metric itself is
  reliable (0.86), with negligible shared-bona-pool dependence.

  What does not survive scrutiny

  - ASVspoof21 "pre-registered" P3 (ρ=0.60/0.64): the registered primary (P1)
  failed and P3 is a promoted secondary among 4 predictors; the earlier J2
  prospective failed 9/9; the "pre-registration" was written 63 seconds before
  scoring by the same script. Exact permutation p = 0.034/0.020 → fails every 
  multiplicity correction (Holm 0.08–0.13, BH q=0.134). In its favor: the two
  detector replications are quasi-independent (their hardness profiles correlate
  only ρ=0.21) and bootstrap/jackknife never cross zero — honest framing is
  "promising exploratory result needing one truly held-out confirmation."
  - The 26–33-point "zero-training-cost fusion" gains are misattributed: the
  supervised eval-corpus LDA probe alone beats the fused score (ITW 0.101 vs
  0.161; MLAAD 0.100 vs 0.116), and ~3–4 EER points of the ITW number come from
  bona-speaker leakage across folds (strict speaker-disjoint: 0.193). This is
  supervised adaptation, not zero-cost fusion.
  - The ITW "fusion 0.363→0.292" is mislabeled — 0.292 is the axis alone; actual
  fusion is 0.312 (fusion hurts on ITW), and no committed script generated that
  artifact.
  - "cos(w_MLAAD, w_ASVspoof21)≈0.36" is flatly false (true value −0.14 to
  −0.21; 0.362 is J1's within-MLAAD cos(w_mean,w_lda) transplanted).
  - I1's fine-grained causal claims: with seed as the inference unit, nothing
  survives BH; the "small residual causal C effect" (iso_0.7, p=0.024) has seeds
  disagreeing in sign and should be retracted. The artifact correction (81%
  share) and the sub_top/sub_res directional asymmetry are seed-consistent and
  stand qualitatively.
  - ITW s_orth (ρ=0.534): survives its family correction, but unreported rog12
  is stronger (ρ=−0.561), collinear, and neither survives partialling the other
  — the axis attribution is unsupported; it's one spread/compactness factor.
  - Packaging errors: 8 verified / 6 mislabeled / 5 misleading / 2 contradicted
  across the report's 21 quantitative claims (claims_vs_artifacts.csv) —
  including fig1 printing s_along's R²=0.020 under the sd_along scatter, "58
  speakers" (actually 29 analyzed), and the I2 battery having zero FDR
  survivors.

  The verdict PDF ends with a 7-item prioritized fix list (regenerate all
  numbers programmatically, reframe the prospective and fusion claims, merge
  s_orth/rog12, seed-level I1 inference, report the 0.86 reliability ceiling,
  commit the missing ITW script). The single most valuable next experiment: one
  externally-timestamped confirmation of P3 on genuinely held-out data
  (ASVspoof21 DF or the non-'none' codec conditions) — that would convert the
  weakest headline claim into a defensible one.

● These are two completely different experiments:

  I7 MLAAD axis fusion (0.272 → 0.163) — within-domain
  - Detector trained on MLAAD, evaluated on MLAAD
  - Axis estimated from MLAAD embeddings (corpus-internal, LOSO folds)
  - Fusing detector + axis on MLAAD test data
  - This is clean: both components see the same domain, the axis is unsupervised
  centroid direction, "zero-training-cost" is fair
  - ΔEER negative in all 5 folds × 3 seeds

  "ITW fusion 0.363 → 0.292" — cross-domain transfer
  - Detector trained on ASVspoof/MLAAD, evaluated on ITW data
  - Axis estimated on ITW (different recording conditions, axis has rotated to
  chance-floor cosine vs MLAAD axis)
  - This is the domain-shift fusion claim — the interesting part is whether the
  ITW-internal axis helps a detector that wasn't trained on ITW
  - But the numbers are mislabeled: 0.292 = axis alone on ITW; actual fusion =
  0.312 (fusion is worse than axis alone); and the 26–33 pt gains come from a
  supervised LDA probe that beats fusion anyway

  So the clean result (I7) is within-domain. The cross-domain ITW claim is where
  the mislabeling and misattribution live. They're testing fundamentally
  different things — I7 asks "does the axis help when you know the domain," ITW
  asks "does it help under domain shift" — and only the former was actually
  demonstrated.
