N is small for the acoustic taxonomy (20 real vs. 20 synthetic per family) and the tests are explicitly uncorrected. The paper is honest about this, but reviewers will still discount the strength of the acoustic claims. (Sec. 3, Sec. 6)

Single real corpus (VocalSound). This is the biggest threat to validity because the “genuine” anchor and XTTS references come from the same source. The authors acknowledge possible source leakage. (Sec. 6)

Single detector architecture and a single benchmark (ASVspoof2019-LA). The locality finding is interesting, but it is not yet established as architecture-independent. (Sec. 6)

No confidence intervals for the within-generator AUCs in Fig. 2a.
